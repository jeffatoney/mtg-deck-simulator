## Counter-payment disposition

The accepted decision is `MODEL_BOTH_LEGAL_CONTROLLED_PLAYER_OUTCOMES`.

Stage 3 acceptance requires:

- PAY and DECLINE are separate when both are legal.
- Only DECLINE is available when payment is impossible.
- No decision is invented for an unmodeled opponent.
- The selected outcome replays exactly.
- Fresh policy recomputation reproduces the same semantic selection.
- Actual payment is validated by the rules engine.
- Payment feasibility is determined through `mtg_kernel.resource_payment` / `mtg_kernel.resource_sources`.
- The PR #99 full-executor deep-copy payment planner is not canonical and is not ported.
