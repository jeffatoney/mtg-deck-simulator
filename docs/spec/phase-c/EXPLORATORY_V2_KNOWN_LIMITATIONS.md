# Exploratory V2 Known Limitations

1. The bounded evaluator intentionally stops speculative continuation before a hidden-information boundary. It therefore values draw, scry, look, shuffle, tutor/search, transmute, typecycle, and impulse effects through visible-state features and package/tutor value rather than by reading the actual future library order.
2. Competent continuation is limited to one exploratory deviation followed by at most eight frozen STANDARD actions while the controlled player retains priority and no hidden-information boundary is crossed. It does not simulate arbitrary future turn trees.
3. The interaction-discovery arm canonicalizes strategic consequences from public action identity, purpose, tags, targets, and public metadata. Two lines that are strategically distinct only because of hidden object identity are intentionally not distinguished.
4. Multi-card strategic choices whose complete combination set exceeds the bounded evidence limit are delegated to frozen STANDARD and recorded with the pruning reason `MULTI_CARD_SELECTION_DELEGATED_TO_FROZEN_STANDARD`. Single-card tutor, look/select, discard, and similar choices are evaluated candidate-by-candidate.
5. The V2 runner currently keeps the frozen STANDARD league mulligan decision as the baseline opening-hand policy. V2 decision evidence begins with in-game strategic choices. A new pilot must not be authorized until directed mulligan choice coverage is either implemented or explicitly excluded by owner authorization.
6. Package progress is a secondary public-state feature and does not redefine legal deterministic full-table access. `ComboAccessTracker` remains authoritative for deterministic-access measurement.
7. Diagnostic seeds are implementation-validation seeds only. They are prohibited from future pilot tuning and are not pilot holdout seeds.
